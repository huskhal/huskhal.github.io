<section class="section-block">
  <h2>Research themes</h2>
  <div class="feature-grid">
    <article class="feature-card">
      <h3>Medication safety and aging</h3>
      <p>Studying how medication use patterns affect older adults, especially when routine prescribing decisions have cognitive or functional consequences.</p>
    </article>
    <article class="feature-card">
      <h3>Delirium and anticholinergic burden</h3>
      <p>Examining how cumulative exposure to anticholinergic medications may shape delirium risk and outcomes in vulnerable hospitalized populations.</p>
    </article>
    <article class="feature-card">
      <h3>Oncology supportive care and disparities</h3>
      <p>Using observational data to understand how supportive care medications are used, who receives them, and where disparities emerge.</p>
    </article>
    <article class="feature-card">
      <h3>Real-world evidence methods</h3>
      <p>Applying causal inference, target trial emulation, and careful data-source selection to make observational evidence more useful and credible.</p>
    </article>
  </div>
</section>
