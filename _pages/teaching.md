  <h2>Teaching as a form of translation</h2>
  <p>I enjoy making difficult ideas easier to understand. In teaching, mentoring, and collaborative work, I try to turn complex methods and clinical questions into forms that are clear, useful, and intellectually honest.</p>
</section>
